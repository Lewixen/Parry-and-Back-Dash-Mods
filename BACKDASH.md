# Back Dash

Brings back the back dash that was cut from Hollow Knight's beta.

Dash backwards without turning around: the Knight keeps facing the same way, so you can back off from an enemy and keep your eyes (and your nail) on it.

## How to use it

Press **dash at the moment you turn around**: press the direction behind you, then dash right away. There's a small timing allowance, so the two don't have to be perfectly at the same time.

- Works on the ground and in the air.
- Same distance, speed and cooldown as the normal dash.
- Shares its cooldown with the normal dash and with Shade Cloak: using one puts the other on cooldown too.
- With **Shade Cloak**, the back dash becomes a back shadow dash, with the same invulnerability.
- Uses its own hand-drawn frames: 7 for the normal back dash and 7 for the back shadow dash.

## Installation

The easiest way is [Lumafly](https://themulhima.github.io/Lumafly/): search for **Back Dash** and install it.

To install by hand:
1. Install the Hollow Knight Modding API.
2. Download `BackDash.zip` from [Releases](../../releases) and extract it into `Hollow Knight/hollow_knight_Data/Managed/Mods/BackDash`.

Back Dash doesn't need any other mod. It works on its own or together with [Parry Mod](README.md).

## Notes

- If you use a Custom Knight skin, the back dash shows the original Knight, because it uses new hand-drawn frames that skins don't include.
